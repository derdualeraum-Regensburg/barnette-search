# v0.8.0 - First public research release

This release makes the research code, corrected manuscript, and available
reconstructed certificates publicly accessible. The manuscript is a draft and
has not been peer-reviewed.

- Expanded the complete Hamiltonian-cycle classification proof, correcting the
  zero-difference rung claim and the exclusion of diagonal connector words.
- Added independent finite proof checks, explicit missing-data skips, a cube
  test fixture, configurable result roots, and Windows/Linux CI.
- Added MIT licensing for original software, CC BY 4.0 for original research
  content, citation metadata, and third-party notices.
- Removed the accidentally committed external drawing-program binaries and
  compiler caches from public Git history; original research identities and
  certificate manifests remain intact.
- Provides reconstructed certificates for 55 graphs through order 24,
  39 of 57 graphs at order 26, and three graphs at order 32. The latter two
  collections are incomplete and establish individual values only.

The original external proof packages through order 36 and the prediction/lift
packages were lost in an accidental drive deletion. Their historical results
remain documented, but full public reproduction above order 24 is not currently
possible. See `docs/data_availability.md` for scope and verification commands.

Assets: `barnette-reconstructed-certificates-v0.8.0.zip`,
`barnette-manuscript-v0.8.0.pdf`, and `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

Validation: all 97 reconstructed graph certificates passed independent
standard-library verification, including complete Hamiltonian-cycle enumeration
and exact lower/upper bounds (527.83 seconds, four workers, Python 3.12.10).
The local software suite passed with 368 tests and 10 explicit skips. The
18-page manuscript was rebuilt and visually checked. Per-graph results and
verifier hashes are recorded in `docs/releases/v0.8.0-verification.json` and in
the certificate archive.
