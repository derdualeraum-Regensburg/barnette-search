\section{Hamiltonian-cycle structure}
\label{sec:hamiltonian-cycles}

The complete Hamiltonian-cycle universes of the five primary double-ladder
members from orders 20 through 36 were encoded using four connector bits, rung
bits, two rail strings per ladder, four-bit cell states, and adjacent-cell
transitions.  Complete universes and cycle counts are also certified for the
three smaller, face-degenerate members, but those three were not included in
the detailed tile-state artifact.  The resulting finite pattern is particularly
simple.

\begin{computationalresult}[Certified cycle counts]
\label{res:cycle-counts}
For each certified graph in
\[
 D(1,1),D(3,1),D(3,3),D(5,3),D(5,5),D(7,5),D(7,7),D(9,7),
\]
the complete Hamiltonian-cycle universe has size
\[
 |\Hcal(D(a,b))|=ab+5.
\]
For each of the five state-encoded members from \(D(5,3)\) through \(D(9,7)\),
every cycle belongs to exactly one of three observed classes: one all-rails
cycle, \(ab\) cycles determined by one turn cell in each ladder, and four
two-connector exceptions.
\end{computationalresult}

Here is the local state mechanism behind the finite classification.  Number the
connectors in the order displayed in \cref{eq:connectors}.  A cycle crosses the
four-edge cut between the two ladders an even, nonzero number of times.  With
all four connectors present, the degree-two equations along one ladder force
the top and bottom rail decisions to agree cell by cell.  In every certified
universe, excluding an internal closed component leaves either the all-rails
state or a single omitted rail pair bounded by two selected rungs.  The latter
is a turn at one of the ladder cells.  Two straight ladders give the all-rails
cycle; two turned ladders give one cycle for each independent pair of turn
positions.

With exactly two connectors, the four observed Hamiltonian connector words are
\[
 1100,\qquad 0011,\qquad 1010,\qquad 0101,
\]
each with one forced completion for odd ladder lengths.  The remaining words
\(1001\) and \(0110\) do not occur in the certified complete universes.

\begin{observation}[Status of the general count]
\label{obs:cycle-formula-status}
The emitted state data exhaustively establish \cref{res:cycle-counts} for the
eight analyzed members.  They strongly suggest that the same degree recurrence
is a symbolic proof for all positive odd \(a,b\), but the present artifact
package checks complete finite universes rather than a separately formalized
parameterized recurrence.  Accordingly, this manuscript does not yet promote
\(|\Hcal(D(a,b))|=ab+5\) to a theorem for the infinite family.  The smaller
certified members support the count but not the detailed state classification.
\end{observation}
