\section{Reproducibility and independent verification}
\label{sec:reproducibility}

\paragraph{Public data availability.}
The original external packages were lost in an accidental deletion of the
research drive.  Release v0.8.0 provides reconstructed graph packages for all
55 graphs in the nonempty censuses through order 24, 39 of the 57 graphs at
order 26, and three graphs at order 32.  Its standard-library release verifier
checks hashes, graph structure, complete Hamiltonian universes, and matching
upper and lower proofs.  The order-26 and order-32 sets are incomplete and
support graph-specific results only.  The original complete evidence through
order 36 and the prospective and lift packages remain unavailable; historical
manifest hashes do not substitute for those files.  Downloads and exact
instructions are maintained in \texttt{docs/data\_availability.md} in the
\href{https://github.com/derdualeraum-Regensburg/barnette-search}{public repository}.

The original audit recorded Plantri 5.8 generation of the complete non-isomorphic Barnette censuses through
order 36: 22,263 graphs across the nonempty orders.  Retained artifacts include
canonical graph records, planar embeddings, cycle universes where required,
separating families, and lower certificates.

A standalone Python standard-library verifier reparses the artifacts without
project, graph, or optimization libraries and checks graph and cycle structure,
ordered edge-pair covers, lower certificates, summaries, and manifests.  It
does not independently prove Plantri's enumerative completeness; that premise
rests on the recorded complete invocations, contiguous output, and record
audits.

The four distinct scopes are certificate verification, the census-completeness
premise, software regression, and figure/layout verification.  The public test
suite explicitly skips missing external datasets and unconfigured Plantri or
cubhamg integrations.  A legacy benchmark with Windows-only memory telemetry
is also skipped on other platforms.  These skips do not replace certificate
checks; release validation records report the actual run and skip counts.

The classification proof also has a separate standard-library regression
check.  For all 25 ordered pairs \(a,b\in\{3,5,7,9,11\}\), it enumerates
perfect matchings directly from \(D(a,b)\), retains exactly the connected
complements, and verifies both the predicted complete Hamiltonian-cycle set
and matching upper and lower certificates.  A further 75 parameter pairs
check the constructions without independently enumerating the complete cycle
universe.  These finite checks support, but do not replace, the general proof
in Sections~\ref{sec:hamiltonian-cycles} and~\ref{sec:exact-hsep}.

The layouts were initialized from certified planar embeddings using Gunnar
Brinkmann's drawing program and then refined under explicit structural
constraints.  The abstract graphs, rotation systems, face boundaries, and edge
sets were independently verified.  Use of the program does not imply
endorsement, collaboration, or coauthorship.

The accompanying repository contains the producer, structural analysis, tests,
standalone verifier, and manuscript.  Large immutable packages remain external;
their identifiers, runtimes, manifest statistics, prediction lock, layout
audit, and order-36 identifier are recorded in
\texttt{reproducibility\_supplement.tex}.
