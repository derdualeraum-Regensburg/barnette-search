\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The certified computation determines \(\MB(n)\) for every nonempty Barnette
census from order 8 through order 36, including all ties and the unique
order-36 value 49.  Its proof objects are explicit: upper certificates are
Hamiltonian-cycle families, and lower certificates are either packings or
complete-universe exhaustive exclusions checked by a standalone verifier.

The finite maximizers reveal two distinct structural regimes.  The unique
maximizers at orders divisible by four lie in the explicitly defined
double-ladder family and are connected by one facial square insertion.  The
tied orders instead exhibit a quadrilateral 3-cycle component and a branching
collection of ladder and cap expansions.  These facts are certified over the
analyzed graphs.  The proposed general Hamiltonian-cycle and hsep formulas, and
the suggested extremal continuation, remain open until parameterized proofs and
new order-level certificates are supplied.  Three prediction-locked tests beyond
the census range confirm the graph-specific formulas for (D(9,9)),
(D(11,9)), and (D(11,11)), but do not establish that any of those graphs is
extremal at its order.

The immediate mathematical priorities are to formalize the general ladder
state recurrence, construct hsep certificates that lift under square insertion,
and classify the tied boundary states.  Before circulation as a research claim,
the manuscript also requires expert review of the direct 3-connectivity proof
and a systematic literature review of all related separation notions and graph
operations.
