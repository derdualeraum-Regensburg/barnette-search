\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The original computation reported \(\MB(n)\) for every nonempty Barnette
census through order 36, including all ties and the unique order-36 value 49.
After loss of its original external packages, public independent reconstruction
currently covers all graphs through order 24 and selected larger graphs.
Restoring the remaining evidence is necessary to reproduce the higher-order
census claims.

For odd \(a,b\ge3\), the double ladder \(D(a,b)\) has one all-rails
Hamiltonian cycle, \(ab\) turn cycles, and four connector exceptions.  Matching
symbolic packing and separating-family constructions prove
\(\hsep(D(a,b))=((a+2)(b+2)-1)/2\) throughout this infinite family.

The original prediction-locked computations reported agreement with the cycle and separation formulas on
three larger family members, but no census-level conclusion follows at orders
40, 44, or 48.  Global extremality beyond order 36 remains open, as does a
structural explanation of the tied branch at orders congruent to two modulo
four.
