\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The recalculated public package certifies \(\MB(n)\) for every nonempty Barnette
census through order 36, including all ties and the unique order-36 value 49.
Its independently checked manifests record the package's file inventory and
hashes.

For odd \(a,b\ge3\), the double ladder \(D(a,b)\) has one all-rails
Hamiltonian cycle, \(ab\) turn cycles, and four connector exceptions.  Matching
symbolic packing and separating-family constructions prove
\(\hsep(D(a,b))=((a+2)(b+2)-1)/2\) throughout this infinite family.

New graph-specific certificates agree with the cycle and separation formulas on
three larger family members, but no census-level conclusion follows at orders
40, 44, or 48.  Global extremality beyond order 36 remains open, as does a
structural explanation of the tied branch at orders congruent to two modulo
four.


The three-edge splice lemma in Section~\ref{sec:quantitative-splice} provides
a constructive bound outside the double-ladder family, with an explicit
reduction based on edge covers in the three boundary states. It propagates
finite separating families through a decomposition and gives a factor-two
bound for cube insertion. Finiteness for undecomposed factors and a sharp
order-level bound remain separate problems, so this composition rule does
not settle the extremal conjecture or Barnette's conjecture.
