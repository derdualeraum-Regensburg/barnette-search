\section{Prospective confirmations beyond order 36}
\label{sec:prospective}

Before the general proofs were completed, a timestamped, read-only prediction
record froze the following values before any target graph was constructed or
analyzed:
\[
\begin{array}{c|rr}
G & |\Hcal(G)| & \hsep(G)\\ \hline
D(9,9) & 86 & 60\\
D(11,9) & 104 & 71\\
D(11,11) & 126 & 84.
\end{array}
\]

All six frozen values were subsequently confirmed exactly by explicit primal
and matching packing certificates and independent verification; the three
graphs appear in Figure~\ref{fig:double-ladder-family}.  Their values now also
follow from Theorems~\ref{thm:cycle-formula} and
\ref{thm:exact-double-ladder}.  No complete Barnette census was run at order
40, 44, or 48.  In particular, the graph-specific identity
\(\hsep(D(9,9))=60\) does not imply \(\MB(40)=60\), and no order-level
extremal claim is made for any of the three orders.
