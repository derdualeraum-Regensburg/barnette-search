\section{The reported finite extremal sequence}
\label{sec:sequence}

\begin{computationalresult}[Original computation through order 36]
\label{res:sequence}
The original complete Plantri 5.8 censuses and certificate audit reported
the values in \cref{tab:extremal-sequence}.  Across the nonempty orders, 22,263
non-isomorphic graphs were reported audited, with exact maximum and tie counts
and strict upper certificates for all nonmaximizers.  The current public
reconstruction verifies the complete censuses through order 24; the lost
original evidence above that order has not been fully restored.
\end{computationalresult}

Every cubic graph has even order, since the degree-sum identity gives
\(3|V|=2|E|\).  Hence all odd orders from 9 through 35 are impossible.  The
complete order-10 Plantri census is empty.  These undefined entries are not
optimization failures.

The originally reported unique order-36 maximizer has \(\hsep=49\) and 68
Hamiltonian cycles, consistent with the general double-ladder theorem below.
The original audit recorded a 49-cycle primal certificate, a matching packing,
and covers of size at most 48 for all 15,373 competitors.  Those complete
order-level certificates are currently unavailable.  Its canonical identifier
is recorded in the reproducibility supplement.

Matching packings certify the displayed maximizers at orders
\(8,12,16,20,24,28,32,36\).  At orders \(14,18,22,26,30,34\), at least one
displayed exact result uses exhaustive set-cover exclusion over a certified
complete cycle universe instead.  This distinction concerns the form of the
lower certificate, not the exactness of the result.

\end{statusnote}
