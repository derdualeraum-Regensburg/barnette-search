\section{The certified finite extremal sequence}
\label{sec:sequence}

\begin{computationalresult}[Recalculated complete census through order 36]
\label{res:sequence}
The release v0.9.0 Plantri 5.8 censuses and certificate audit give the values in
\cref{tab:extremal-sequence}.  Across the nonempty orders, 22,263
non-isomorphic graphs are audited, with exact maximum and tie counts and strict
upper certificates for all nonmaximizers.  A standalone verifier checks the
complete package independently of the producer.
\end{computationalresult}

Every cubic graph has even order, since the degree-sum identity gives
\(3|V|=2|E|\).  Hence all odd orders from 9 through 35 are impossible.  The
complete order-10 Plantri census is empty.  These undefined entries are not
optimization failures.

The unique order-36 maximizer has \(\hsep=49\) and 68
Hamiltonian cycles, consistent with the general double-ladder theorem below.
The recalculation supplies a 49-cycle primal certificate, a matching packing,
and covers of size at most 48 for all 15,373 competitors.  Its canonical
identifier is recorded in the reproducibility supplement.

Matching packings certify the displayed maximizers at orders
\(8,12,16,20,24,28,32,36\).  At orders \(14,18,22,26,30,34\), at least one
displayed exact result uses exhaustive set-cover exclusion over a certified
complete cycle universe instead.  This distinction concerns the form of the
lower certificate, not the exactness of the result.
