\subsection*{Layout study}

The layout study evaluated 12,672 candidate variants and exercised all 400
cap-selecting directed edges.  The structural face \texttt{B\_rail\_0} was
selected as the exterior face for all eleven family members.  Constrained
refinement produced horizontal ladder-A rails, boundary-following ladder B,
convex outer faces, consistent insertion orientation, and zero crossings.  The
unweighted family transition cost decreased from 13.6693 for independent
minima to 1.52964.  Independent verification passed the graph, rotation,
face-boundary, edge-set, label, crossing, composite, and manifest checks.
